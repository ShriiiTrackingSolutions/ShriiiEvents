About Shriii Events - Vadodara's Premier Event Planners

Welcome to Shriii Events, Vadodara's most distinguished and trusted event planning and decoration company. We are passionate storytellers who transform your special moments into timeless memories that last forever. With over a decade of excellence in event management, we have proudly served 500+ clients across Vadodara, Baroda, and entire Gujarat.

At Shriii Events, we believe that every celebration deserves meticulous attention, creative brilliance, and flawless execution. Whether it's the grandest wedding in Vadodara, an intimate engagement ceremony, a milestone corporate event, or a joyous birthday celebration, we bring your vision to life with precision, elegance, and passion.

Our journey began with a simple yet powerful mission: to make event planning stress-free and memorable for every client. Today, we stand as Vadodara's go-to event planners, renowned for our innovative decoration concepts, personalized service, and unwavering commitment to excellence.

Our Story - A Decade Of Excellence

A Journey Of Passion, Growth, And Creating Magical Moments.

Our team has grown to include award-winning designers, experienced coordinators, skilled decorators, and vendor management experts who work tirelessly to exceed client expectations. Every event we create is a testament to our commitment to quality, creativity, and customer satisfaction.

2015

The Begining (2015)

Shriii Events was founded in 2015 with a vision to revolutionize event planning in Vadodara. Starting from a small team of passionate designers and coordinators, we embarked on a mission to create extraordinary celebrations that reflect our clients' unique stories and personalities.

2018

Growth & Recognition (2017-2020)

Within a few years, our innovative approach to event decoration and meticulous planning earned us recognition across Gujarat. We expanded our services from wedding planning to include corporate events, birthday parties, baby showers, and engagement ceremonies. Our portfolio grew to include collaborations with Vadodara's finest venues, including Grand Mercure Vadodara Surya Palace, Statue of Unity Tent City, Kabir Farms, and premium banquet halls across the city.

2022

Excellence & Innovation (2021-Present)

Today, Shriii Events stands as Vadodara's premier event managemen company with 500+ successful events, 200+ dream weddings, and 15 corporate celebrations. We continue to set trends in event decoration introduce contemporary themes, and leverage technology to deliver seamless experiences.

Our Event Planning Approach

How We Turn Your Vision into Reality

At Shriii Events, we follow a systematic yet personalized approach that ensures every detail is carefully planned and flawlessly executed. Here's how we work:

Initial Consultation (Free)

We begin with a detailed discussion to understand your vision, requirements, preferences, budget, and expectations. This helps us create a foundation for perfect planning.

Concept Development

Our creative team develops customized concepts, mood boards, theme ideas, and decoration mockups tailored to your style and event type.

Detailed Proposal

You receive a comprehensive proposal with service breakdown, venue options, vendor recommendations, timeline, and transparent pricing with no hidden costs.

Planning & Coordination

Once approved, we begin meticulous planning including venue booking, vendor coordination, timeline creation, logistics arrangement, and complete event roadmap.

Design & Decoration Setup

Our skilled team transforms the venue with stunning decoration, floral arrangements, lighting installation, stage setup, and complete ambiance creation.

Event Execution

On the event day, our professional coordinators manage everything on-site ensuring smooth execution, timing adherence, vendor coordination, and seamless experience.
